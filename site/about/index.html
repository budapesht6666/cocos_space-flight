The game

One finger

Drag anywhere to fly; the guns fire by themselves. Your thumb is free for the Special: Nova Bomb, Overdrive, Phase Shield or Time Warp.

3 sectors, 9 missions

Outer Ring, Ice Moons and Nebula Drift: asteroid fields, mines, swarms and laser wings, six mini-bosses and three bosses — Warden, Hive Mother and Leviathan.

An arsenal to build

Six main guns from Pulse Cannon to Tesla Arc, four secondary weapons, three drones and four Specials. Power up mid-mission, upgrade for credits in the hangar.

Three ships

Spitfire, Executioner and Striker — each with its own hull, shield, speed and firepower, in three paint jobs.

Medals and difficulty

Four medals in every mission — Hunter, Exterminator, Rescuer, Untouchable — and four difficulties up to Nightmare, where elite enemies come out.

Plays anywhere

iPhone, Android or a computer's browser, nothing to download. Installed to the home screen it runs full screen and offline. Progress stays on your device.

Install it

iPhone and iPad

  1. Tap Play now to open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share — on iOS 26 it is under ··· next to the address bar.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add.

Start it from the new icon: full screen, no browser bars, works offline.

Android

  1. Tap Play now to open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ in the top corner.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen → Install.

The game gets its own icon and opens full screen.

Computer

  1. Just press Play now: it runs in any modern browser.
  2. Fly with the mouse, the arrows or WASD.
  3. Space fires the Special, Esc pauses.

Chrome and Edge can install it as an app too: the install icon in the address bar.

Progress is saved in the browser on that device. On iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own save, apart from Safari — install first, then play.
